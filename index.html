Junior High Volleyball

UIL Texas · 7th & 8th grade girls

Match
Best of 3, rally scoring. By mutual consent the third set may be played even when the same team has won the first two.
Sets 1 & 2
To 25, win by 2, capped at 30 — at 29–29 the next point wins.
Third set
To 15, win by 2, capped at 20. Districts may instead play the third set to 25 (cap 30).
Timeouts
Two per team per set.
Let serve
A serve that touches the net and lands in is live and playable.
Tournaments
Pool play is usually mini games to 15, capped at 20. Bracket matches follow the regular format.
Sides
Teams switch benches between sets. Use Swap sides to keep the board matching the court.

Referee signals

The call, then what the whistle means

Summarized from the UIL volleyball rally scoring regulations. District and tournament directors can set their own variations, so confirm with the host before first serve.
